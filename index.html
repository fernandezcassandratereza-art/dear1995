How it works

Five small steps, one link.

No sign-up forms, no passwords. Open the editor and start writing.

  1. 01

    Write it

    Their name, your words, in a handwritten font. Filipino, English, any language.

  2. 02

    Add memories

    Upload photos from your gallery. They become Polaroids with your captions.

  3. 03

    Choose a song

    Add your own MP3. It plays from a little cassette when they open the letter.

  4. 04

    Make it yours

    Six vintage themes, colors, paper, pressed flowers, and hidden surprises.

  5. 05

    Seal & send

    Publish and get one unique link. Send it by Messenger, text, or a QR code. They open it on any phone — no account needed.
